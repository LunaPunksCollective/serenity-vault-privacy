Serenity Vault — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-10

Serenity Vault is a watch-only Terra Classic portfolio app developed under the LunaPunksCollective brand. The publisher responsible for this app is PAULO ALEXANDRE MENDES D'ABOIM PINTO. Contact: aboimpinto@gmail.com.

What the app does

You enter a public Terra Classic address to see balances and estimated values. The app does not create an account, hold funds, request private keys or seed phrases, or sign transactions.

Information saved on your device

The app saves the public address you choose and its most recent successful balance snapshot in app-private storage. The snapshot includes balances, pending rewards, prices, timestamps, block height and the node used. These records let the app remember your selection and show explicitly labelled saved data when offline.

The app does not include advertising, analytics or automatic crash-reporting SDKs. It does not request access to contacts, photos, precise location, camera, microphone or advertising identifiers. It uses the internet to retrieve public blockchain data and prices.

Requests to external services

On startup with a saved address, and when you request a refresh, the app sends that address in HTTPS requests to Terra Classic node providers. A fallback provider may be tried if a request fails. The configured providers are Binodes (api-lunc-lcd.binodes.com), PublicNode (terra-classic-lcd.publicnode.com) and Hexxagon (lcd.terra-classic.hexxagon.io).

The app requests LUNC and USTC USD prices from CoinGecko (api.coingecko.com). It does not include your wallet address in CoinGecko price requests.

These services receive your network IP address and request information such as the requested path, time, and an app/version user-agent. A blockchain address can be associated with a person and reveal public financial activity; public availability does not make it anonymous.

Serenity Vault has no developer-operated backend receiving these requests. External providers operate their own infrastructure and may retain request logs under their policies. We do not promise that their processing is temporary or that they keep no logs. Their use and retention of data must be considered separately from the app's local storage.

Why information is used

The address is used to retrieve the selected portfolio, prices are used to estimate its USD value, and the local snapshot is used to restore the last view. The app does not sell data or use it for advertising, profiling or cross-app tracking.

Retention and deletion

The selected address and snapshot remain on your device until replaced, removed with Forget wallet, cleared through Android's app settings, or removed by uninstalling. Changing the address clears the earlier snapshot. The app disables Android cloud backup and transfer of its saved preferences.

Forgetting a wallet removes the app's local copy. It does not remove public blockchain records or delete external providers' logs. We cannot erase immutable blockchain records or independently control another provider's retention. Contact us about data-handling questions; requests concerning a provider's logs may need to be directed to that provider.

Security

Network requests use HTTPS and Android app-private storage protects local preferences from ordinary access by other apps. No secret that could authorize a blockchain transaction is stored by Serenity Vault. Device compromise, modified operating systems or access to an unlocked phone can affect these protections.

External links and support

Opening a GitHub or CoinGecko link uses your browser or another app, which has its own privacy practices. Support and privacy inquiries can be sent to aboimpinto@gmail.com. This inbox uses Google's Gmail service. Your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send are used to respond to your inquiry. Support correspondence is retained while needed to handle the inquiry and any related follow-up or legal obligations. You can request deletion by emailing the same address; records that must be retained will be explained in the response. We do not need seed phrases or private keys to provide support.

Visiting this policy page

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. The page does not add analytics scripts, advertising or a contact form. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for its processing practices. Sending an email is also subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Changes and questions

This policy will be updated when the app's data handling changes. The effective date identifies the current policy. Contact aboimpinto@gmail.com with privacy questions.
